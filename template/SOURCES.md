`cbam-template-v2.1.1.xlsx` is the official EU file, unmodified. It is renamed only.

`templateMap.json` is generated from it by `tools/gen_template_map.py`. It lists every cell the app may write: unlocked, non-formula input cells. It also records each cell's type, the template's own option lists, and the block capacities. Re-run the script whenever the template changes.

The official filled examples, including "Steel 3 Screws and nuts", come from the examples pack on the same page (dated 20 June 2024). They are kept in `reference/examples/` and are not committed.
